## 2026-09-11T05:37:55.194Z

The purpose of this component on the enrollment home pager is to show every day in the enrollment and visually communicate completion for that day. The time on the card is the time of day the lesson was sent to members of the group and/or made available. The purple number above the time is the day number of the enrollment.
