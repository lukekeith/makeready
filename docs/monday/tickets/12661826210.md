# missing elements (Bible reader verse numbers vanish after chapter swipe)

## Problem
In the iPhone app's Bible passage picker (the "Highlight passage" reader overlay, reached from the exegesis editor's Add passage flow — also presented by the Read editor's add-bible-verse flow), swiping horizontally to an adjacent chapter makes the **gutter verse numbers disappear**, and they stay gone for every chapter viewed afterward in that overlay session. Only reopening the overlay restores them. "Fixed" observably looks like: after any left/right chapter swipe completes, the new chapter renders with its verse numbers in the left gutter, and subsequent chapters keep them.

## Reports (verbatim)
- (2026-07-29) "When adding exegesis, add passage, selecting the passage and then swiping left to see the previous chapter, the verse numbers go away for all passages"

## Screenshots
- `image.jpeg` (10:33 local ≈ 15:33 UTC 2026-07-29, iPhone) — Bible reader overlay in "Highlight passage" mode: NASB version chip + book-list button + "Exodus 11:1" in the search bar, back chevron, disabled "Select" link. Chapter heading "Exodus 11" with justified serif body text and **no verse numbers in the left gutter** (empty margin circled in yellow). Identifies the surface as `BibleReaderOverlay` reader mode after a chapter swipe.

## Affected areas

### In scope
- **Platform:** iphone (leader authoring — passage picker overlay)
- **Screen:** Bible reader overlay — `iphone/MakeReady/Pages/Bible/BibleReaderOverlay.swift`
- **Component(s):**
  - `completeChapterTransition(to:chapter:direction:)` (~line 603): animates `verseCircleContainer` to `alpha 0` + off-screen `transform` alongside the outgoing text, then in the completion restores only `readerTextView` (transform reset line ~618, alpha 1 line ~628) while re-asserting `verseCircleContainer.alpha = 0` (line ~620) and never resetting its transform.
  - `layoutVerseCircles()` (~line 1064): rebuilds the number views but never restores the container's `alpha`/`transform`, so the rebuilt numbers render inside an invisible, translated container.
  - Contrast: the cancelled-swipe path (~lines 578-580) DOES restore `transform = .identity` / `alpha = 1` — the completed-transition path is the only one missing it.
- **Endpoints:** none (UI-only; chapter content loads from the local `BibleCacheManager`)

### Explicitly out of scope
- `iphone/MakeReady/Components/Content/ExegesisVerseView.swift` and `SelectableLockedBlockView.swift` — sibling consumers of `BibleVerseTextLayout.layoutVerseBadges` (exegesis preview pane, read-activity locked blocks); their badge rendering has no swipe transition and is unaffected.
- `iphone/MakeReady/Components/Content/BibleVerseTextLayout.swift` — shared layout helper used by three surfaces; the bug is NOT here (it's the overlay's container alpha/transform), do not touch.
- `EditExegesisActivityPage.swift` / `EditReadActivityPage.swift` — they merely present the overlay; no change needed.
- Web LeaderApp Bible picker twin (`client/` leader-bible pane) — separate implementation, no swipe-chapter feature; unaffected.

### Cross-app impact
None — UI-only change in one iPhone overlay file. Both presenting editors (Read + Exegesis) inherit the fix automatically.

## Root cause
`BibleReaderOverlay.swift` `completeChapterTransition` (~603-639): the swap animation hides `verseCircleContainer` (alpha 0, translated by ±width) but the completion only resurrects `readerTextView`; the circle container is left invisible and off-screen. The subsequent `loadReaderContent → layoutVerseCircles` rebuilds the verse-number subviews without resetting the container's alpha/transform, so every later chapter renders numbers into a hidden container. Session-sticky until the overlay is dismissed and reopened.

## Verification
1. **Code-level proof:** `BibleReaderOverlay.swift` — grep `verseCircleContainer`: alpha/transform are restored only in the swipe-cancel branch (~578-580), never after `completeChapterTransition` (620 re-asserts alpha 0) nor in `layoutVerseCircles`.
2. **Manual repro (simulator):** Edit an exegesis activity → Add passage → pick any book/chapter to enter the reader → swipe left or right to an adjacent chapter → observe the new chapter (and all subsequent ones, including via the grid) has no gutter verse numbers; reopening the overlay restores them. After the fix, numbers survive every swipe.
