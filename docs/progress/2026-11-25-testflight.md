This build is all about video. You can now record or import a short video from the + menu or inside a lesson, add text and captions, name it with AI suggestions, and find it in Library. Lesson video activities now show, play and delete like real videos. Please test on a real phone with sound on, since sound was not checked before this build.

**Video recorder**
Tap + > Record a video. The right side has Flip, Countdown, Length, Filters and Teleprompter.
How to test: record three short clips, delete the last one, then record again. Try a 3 s countdown with Sound on and then with the silent switch on. In Length, choose Specify length and type 130 (you should see 1:30) or 500 (it should cap at 2:00). Add teleprompter text or use a note.
Expected: every panel slides up and closes with a swipe or a tap outside it. Recording stops on its own at the length you set. If you close the teleprompter empty, it stays off. A call or locking the phone pauses the recording and keeps your clips. Imported clips over 2:00 ask you to trim first.

**Review and upright videos**
Tap the video to play or pause it. Playback stops at the end.
How to test: record with the front camera and with the back camera, then save both.
Expected: both play upright in Library and in lessons, never upside down or mirrored.

**Video editor**
On Review, tap Edit to add text layers and captions.
How to test: add two text layers, tap Captions, reword one caption, set when each layer shows on the timeline, then tap Done and Save.
Expected: the saved video shows the text at the right moments, and the transcript has your corrected wording. Trims stop at the clip's edges. Tapping empty video deselects a layer, or plays and pauses when nothing is selected. Cancel only asks to confirm if you changed something.

**Name your video**
How to test: record about 20 seconds of speech from the + menu and save it.
Expected: a "Name your video" page shows Title and Description, and three AI suggestions appear within seconds. Tapping one fills both fields, and the Library row then shows that title. If you leave the title empty, the video saves as "Video N". When you're offline, the page says "Couldn't get suggestions" but saving still works. Lesson recordings skip this page.

**Library videos**
Videos now appear in Library with notes and memos, in one list with no date headers. Each video row shows a thumbnail, and the Type filter has a Videos option.
How to test: open a video, rename it, and read its transcript. Then delete one video that isn't in any lesson and one that is.
Expected: the first video is deleted. The second leaves Library but stays in its lessons.

**Swipe to delete**
How to test: swipe a note, a memo and a video all the way to the left.
Expected: the row fills red and folds away without bouncing back. If you cancel the confirmation, the row comes back.

**Lesson video activities**
How to test: in Edit lesson, add a Video activity and record. Then tap it, press and hold it, and choose Delete.
Expected: the page shows the video's picture. A tap plays and pauses it. Holding opens Replace and Delete. Delete offers Remove from lesson or Delete video everywhere, and Delete everywhere is disabled when another lesson or a published lesson uses the video. Video slides on the group and enrollment rails show the video's picture.

**Also**
- A retried upload that works no longer flashes a red error.
- Filters no longer dims the camera picture.
- The text size control is now a thin line with a dot, in both editors.

Please focus testing on recording and saving from the + menu (including naming), the editor's captions and text, upright playback after upload, and deleting videos from lessons and Library.
