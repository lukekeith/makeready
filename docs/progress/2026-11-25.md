## 📱 iPhone app — user-facing

### Video recorder (+ menu → Record a video) — new, highest risk

- **Full-screen camera recorder** with a right rail: **Flip, Countdown, Length, Filters,
  Teleprompter**. Rail labels show briefly, then fade; tapping a tool brings them back.
  **QA:** + → Record a video. Camera and microphone are asked for on first use; deny them in
  Settings and confirm the camera area explains it and offers **Open Settings**.
- **Segments.** Record, stop, record again — the progress bar shows the segments with gaps.
  **Delete last clip** (left of the record button) removes only the last segment.
  **QA:** record 3 segments, delete the last, record again; the total time must be right.
- **Countdown panel** (Off · 3 s · 10 s) with a **Sound** switch, off by default and dimmed while
  Countdown is Off. The beep is silenced by the ring/silent switch.
  **QA:** 3 s countdown with Sound on and the ringer on → numerals and beeps; flip the silent
  switch → numerals only.
- **Length panel:** **Any length (2 min cap)** or **Specify length** with a typed `m:ss` input
  (digits fill from the right, over 2:00 becomes 2:00, under 0:05 becomes 0:05) and an optional
  **End countdown: Off · 3 s · 10 s** (default Off). Recording stops itself at the length.
  **QA:** type `130` → 1:30; type `500` → 2:00; specify 0:15 with a 3 s end countdown and confirm
  the numerals and the auto-stop.
- **Filters** is a slide-up sheet (8 looks + strength), from the rail and from Review; the live
  picture is not dimmed under it.
- **Teleprompter:** type, paste or **Use a note** from Library; **Clear** empties it. It only turns
  on when there is text. Text scrolls near the lens while recording, pauses with the segment and
  resumes on the next one. Speed and size controls.
  **QA:** open the teleprompter, close it empty → it must stay off.
- **All four recorder sheets** (Length, Countdown, Filters, Teleprompter) slide up with a handle,
  dim the screen, and dismiss by swipe or tapping off, like the app's other action menus.
- **Import from Photos** (gallery button, before recording): a clip ≤ 2:00 goes straight to Review;
  a longer clip opens a trim step first.
- **Interruptions:** a call, backgrounding or a camera interruption ends the segment and pauses;
  it never resumes by itself. Recording refuses to start with under ~500 MB free.
  **QA:** start recording, lock the phone or take a call, come back → paused, earlier segments kept.
- **Crash safety:** finished segments survive a force-quit.
  **QA:** record two segments, force-quit, reopen the recorder → Review is restored.

### Review

- Tap the video to play/pause; playback stops at the end (no loop), a tap there replays. A
  scrubber is shown.
- Buttons: **Filters, Edit, Retake** (with confirm), **Save**. In the full-screen recorder, Save
  is a plain checkmark and the step into Review is a forward arrow; inside a lesson, Save is the
  purple check in the "Edit lesson" header.
- **Orientation fix:** exported videos are now drawn upright (rotation and front-camera mirror are
  baked into the pixels), fixing videos that played upside down after upload.
  **QA:** record with the front and the back camera, plain Normal filter, no layers; after upload
  both must play upright in Library and in a lesson.

### Video editor (Review → Edit) — new

- **Text layers** (up to 20) using the activities editor's text tool: font, size, colour,
  background, alignment; drag, pinch, rotate; centre/snap guides and an unsafe-edge band.
- **Captions** tool: transcribes the clip on device ("Transcribing…") and adds one caption per
  spoken segment on one shared track; captions can be reworded, and the corrected wording is
  saved as the video's transcript.
- **Timeline:** one track per text layer, a captions track, a filmstrip, a centre playhead. Trims
  and slides stop at the clip edges and snap to clip edges, other layers and the playhead (with a
  haptic). Selected layer toolbar: **Split · Edit · Delete** (Deselect button and drag-to-delete
  target are gone — tap empty video to deselect; with nothing selected the tap plays/pauses).
- **Cancel / Undo / Done.** Cancel only confirms if something changed. Edits survive a force-quit.
- **Text size control** is now a thin vertical line with a dot on the trailing edge (also in the
  Activities editor's text tool).
- Layers are drawn into the uploaded file.
  **QA:** add two text layers and captions to a ~1:00 clip, set different windows, fix one caption,
  Done → Save. In Library the video must show the text at the right times and the transcript must
  carry the fixed wording.

### Name your video (+ menu recordings only) — new

- After Review's save from the + menu, a **Name your video** page offers Title (80 chars) and
  Description (300 chars) and, within seconds, **3 AI suggestions** (title + description pairs)
  written from what was said. Tapping one fills both fields; typing afterwards is kept;
  Regenerate asks again. The check saves.
- The upload already runs in the background while you name it; the Library row only appears with
  the chosen name.
- Empty title → saves as "Video N". Back returns to Review. Backgrounding the app or closing keeps
  it as "Video N".
- If suggestions fail (offline), the block reads "Couldn't get suggestions" and naming still works.
- Lesson recordings do **not** show this page.
  **QA:** record ~20 s of speech from the + menu → Save → pick a suggestion → check → Library row
  shows that title. Repeat offline (suggestions fail, save still works) and with an empty title.

### Library

- **Videos are a third row kind** beside notes and memos, newest first, one flat list (no more
  date-section headers). Row: video avatar, title, date · duration, and the transcript's first
  line or Transcribing… / Uploading… / Processing…. Rows now show the video thumbnail.
- **Type filter** gains **Videos**; search matches title and transcript.
- **Video detail** (tap a row): player, tap the title to rename, date and duration, transcript,
  Delete in the overflow menu.
- **Delete:** a video used in no lessons → "Delete this video?" and it is deleted. A video used in
  lessons → it stays in those lessons and leaves your Library.
- **Swipe-to-delete fix:** a full swipe now fills the row with red, then collapses it — no bounce
  back. Video rows collapse immediately like notes and memos; a failed delete (or cancelling the
  in-lessons confirm) brings the row back.
  **QA:** full-swipe a note, a memo and a video row; each collapses in place. Cancel the second
  confirm on a lesson-used video → the row returns.
- **Upload error flash fix:** a retried upload that succeeds no longer flashes the red upload
  banner.
  **QA:** record offline, go back online and let the retry run → no red banner flicker.

### Lesson video activities

- **New activity → Video** tile is enabled and opens the recorder inside Edit lesson. After save,
  the activity shows "Uploading…" and attaches itself once the upload finishes, even if the editor
  was closed.
- **Video page shows the video's still** (no more blank canvas with "Video 1"); while the still is
  still processing it appears once ready.
- **Tap plays/pauses inline** (aspect-fill, with sound, stops at the end; tap again replays); the
  play glyph hides while playing and a scrubber appears once loaded.
- **Press and hold (0.5 s)** opens **Replace · Delete**.
- **Delete** asks "Delete this video?": **Remove from lesson** or **Delete video everywhere** (a
  toggle, off each time it opens). Delete everywhere is disabled with "Used in …" when another
  lesson (any version, including published) uses the video, or "Used in a published lesson" for
  a published version without a draft. After removal the editor opens the neighbouring activity.
- **Thumbnails:** video activity slides on the group, enrollment and program rails now show the
  video's still instead of a blank tile.
  **QA:** create a video activity, play/pause, hold → Delete → try both options; check the
  rails for the still; on a video used in two lessons, Delete everywhere must be disabled.

## 🔌 Backend / API — affects in-app behavior

- **`POST /api/videos`** accepts `transcript` / `transcriptSegments`, is idempotent for the same
  owner (duplicate create answers 200 with the row), and creates the org Media row before
  returning, so lessons can attach immediately. Body is strict with size limits.
- **`GET /api/videos/library`** — paged Library video list; **remove-from-Library** endpoint;
  `GET /api/videos/me` now excludes removed videos.
- **`lessonUseCount` and `versionUseCount`** on `GET /api/videos/:id` drive the delete choices.
- **Stream status sync** in the maintenance job moves pending videos to ready; **server fallback
  transcription** from Stream downloads when the phone had no transcript.
- **`POST /api/videos/suggest-details`** — Claude-generated 3 title/description pairs from a
  transcript (20 s timeout).
- **Lesson thumbnails** fall back to the video's still when there is no background or poster.
- New DB columns: `Video` transcript fields and `libraryRemovedAt` (Atlas migration
  `20261122200800_video_transcript_library`). All changes are additive; older app builds keep
  working.
